UNCC Article Chart #2

UNCC News Article Report

The line chart shows how The Leon Levine Foundation’s support for the Levine Scholars Program has steadily grown since its inception. Starting at zero in 2009, cumulative donations rise to about $10 million by 2012, then jump sharply to $30 million around the program’s first major expansion in 2016. A further increase to $40 million by 2020 reflects continued commitment, and the latest $24.9 million gift pushes the total past $53 million in 2025. This upward trajectory underscores the Foundation’s unwavering, long-term investment in the scholars’ success.

Data Source: This data was compiled from UNCC's official news page. This article was published on May 1, 2025, and it discusses a $24.9 million donation from the Leon Levine Foundation to expand and sustain the UNCC Levine Scholars Program. The article highlights the impact of this funding on the university's commitment to providing transformative educational opportunities for students.
