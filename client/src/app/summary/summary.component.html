UNCC Article Chart #1

UNCC News Article Report

The chart reveals that in-state candidates have generally outnumbered out-of-state applicants from 2021 to 2024, but with more pronounced year-to-year swings. In-state numbers rose from 11 in 2021 to 16 in 2022, dipped to 9 in 2023, then surged to 19 in 2024. In contrast, out-of-state candidates showed steadier growth—starting at 7 in 2021, climbing to 9 in 2022, then holding at 10 for both 2023 and 2024. This suggests that while interest from non-residents has steadily increased and stabilized, in-state engagement can vary more dramatically from one year to the next.

Data Source: This data was compiled from UNCC's official news page. This article was published on May 1, 2025, and it discusses a $24.9 million donation from the Leon Levine Foundation to expand and sustain the UNCC Levine Scholars Program. The article highlights the impact of this funding on the university's commitment to providing transformative educational opportunities for students.
